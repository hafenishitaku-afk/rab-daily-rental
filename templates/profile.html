What is it? It is a second lock on your account. Even if someone steals your password, they still can't log in without the code from your phone.

How do I set it up? It takes 2 minutes:

  1. Click the "Enable 2FA" button below.
  2. You will see a square barcode (QR code) on the next screen.
  3. Download a free app on your phone like Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or Authy from your App Store.
  4. Open the app and tap the "+" button to scan the barcode.
  5. Type the 6-digit number the app shows you into the website.
Can't scan the barcode?

⏱️ Note: The 6-digit code in your app changes every 30 seconds. This is normal and keeps your account secure! If a code is rejected during setup, simply wait for the app to generate the next one and try again.

If your camera isn't working (or you are using your phone to view the website), look for the text link on the next screen that says "Can't scan? Enter this code manually".

Click it, and a long text code will appear. Simply copy that code and paste it into your Authenticator app (choose "Enter a setup key" instead of "Scan barcode"). The app will generate your 6-digit codes perfectly!

We will also give you 8 backup codes to print and keep safe, just in case you lose your phone.
