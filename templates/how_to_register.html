📝 How to Register

Everything you need to prepare before starting your registration.

Requirements Checklist

Please have the following ready before you fill in the registration form:

  • ✅ Full Name (as it appears on your ID)
  • ✅ ID / Passport Number
  • ✅ Address
  • ✅ Customer Photo (A clear selfie or photo of your face)
  • ✅ Phone Number
  • ✅ Email Address (Optional, but recommended)
  • ✅ ID / Passport Copy (A photo of the front side where your picture is. You do not need the back side.)

📌 What Happens Next?

  1. Fill in the form and upload your pictures.
  2. Read and tick the Terms & Conditions box.
  3. Sign the digital signature pad.
  4. Click Submit.
  5. You will get a message telling you how to check your application status using your cellphone number.
  6. Once approved, your login details will be sent to you.
  7. If your application succeeds, you must bring your physical ID or Passport with you on the day of your FIRST rental.
  8. After your first rental is complete, you will only need to provide your ID or Passport number for future rentals.
