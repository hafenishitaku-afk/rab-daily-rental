🔒 Privacy Policy

{{ company_name }} — Last updated: {{ last_updated }}

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have over it. We aim to comply with the Namibian Data Protection Act, 2022 (Act 5 of 2022).

Questions about this policy? Contact us at {{ privacy_email }}.

1. What we collect

  • Identity data: full name, ID or passport number, date of birth (if provided)
  • Contact data: phone number, email address, physical address
  • Verification data: scanned copy of your ID/passport, a photo of you taken at registration, updated every 2 weeks
  • Digital signature: the signature you provide when accepting the rental terms
  • Rental activity: which bicycles you rented, when, for how long, where you picked up and returned them
  • Payment data: amount paid, payment method, damage charges
  • Technical data: IP address and device information when staff access your file (kept for security auditing)

2. Why we collect it

  • To verify your identity before renting a bicycle
  • To provide the rental service, calculate charges, and apply deposits correctly
  • To recover damages or investigate disputes
  • To comply with tax and accounting laws
  • To improve security and detect suspicious activity
  • To contact you about your rentals (reminders, overdue notices)

3. Who can see your data

  • Staff can see your name, contact details, verification status, and rental history — needed to serve you.
  • Managers can see the above plus payment and damage records.
  • Administrators can see everything, including full IP addresses in security logs.
  • Nobody else can access your personal information. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Every access to your documents is logged. Full audit trail available on request.

4. How long we keep your data

  • Rental records: kept for {{ retention.rental_records_years }} years (accounting requirement)
  • Payment records: kept for {{ retention.payment_records_years }} years (accounting requirement)
  • ID documents & photos: kept for {{ retention.customer_documents_years }} years after your last rental, then permanently deleted
  • Damage claims: kept for {{ retention.damage_claims_years }} years
  • Login history: kept for {{ retention.login_history_days }} days
  • Audit log: kept for {{ retention.audit_log_days }} days
  • Document view log: kept for {{ retention.document_views_days }} days

5. Your rights

Under the Namibian Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON) plus a human-readable PDF
  • Correction — ask us to fix incorrect information
  • Deletion — request that we delete your account and personal data
  • Objection — object to how we process your data
  • Withdraw consent — at any time

To exercise any of these rights, log in to your account and use the Privacy section, or email us at {{ privacy_email }}.

6. What happens when you ask us to delete your account

  1. You submit a deletion request through your account.
  2. An administrator reviews the request to make sure no outstanding balances or disputes exist.
  3. Once approved, we anonymize your record: your name becomes "Deleted User", contact details are erased, and your ID documents and photo are permanently deleted.
  4. Your rental and payment history is retained in anonymized form (as required by accounting law) so it can no longer be linked to you.
  5. You cannot log in to the system after deletion.

Note: we cannot delete records we are legally required to keep (e.g., tax/accounting records). These are anonymized but not deleted.

7. Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS with strict transport security.
  • Uploaded documents are stored privately and never publicly accessible.
  • Only authenticated staff can access customer records, and every access is logged.
  • Login attempts are rate-limited and suspicious logins trigger admin alerts.
  • Passwords are stored using industry-standard one-way hashing (never plaintext).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Significant changes will be announced to registered customers.
